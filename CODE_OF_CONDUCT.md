# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith, and give people the benefit of the doubt before concluding
  they are being obstructive.
- Be precise about technical disagreement. Review the evidence, not the person.
- Accept that a maintainer may disagree with you, and that you may disagree back.
- Disagree with ideas, not people.

Security research discussions can get heated, because the subject matter
involves people demonstrating how systems can be broken. Keep that professional.
Pointing out that a scenario's oracle is too lenient is useful. Publishing a
working exploit against somebody's production system is not.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation or sustained disruption.
- Personal attacks, slurs, or sexualised language.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Encouraging anyone to break into or test systems they do not own.
- Submitting exploit payloads aimed at real third-party systems in an issue or
  pull request.

## Scope

Applies in project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when
representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers by opening a report through
GitHub's private reporting channel, or by contacting the maintainer directly if
you prefer. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this in good faith may face temporary
or permanent repercussions.
